# figures/archive_2026-02/

**Superseded figures — kept for the record. Do not use in new materials.**

These are the figures used in the February 2026 competition materials (DRSEF, GENIUS Olympiad,
Sigma Xi). They are preserved unchanged so that earlier presentations and papers can be traced.
A September 2026 re-analysis of the raw logs found that several of them carry a wrong gap label,
hand-entered model curves, or a vacuum result that is not supported by the data. The current
figures are in `../paper/` (TJAS 2026 paper) and `../regenerated/`.
